# Mapping the Journey Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 19 November 2026**

Mapping the Journey is a location-based exploration application. This privacy policy explains how the current TestFlight version uses and handles information.

## Location

Mapping the Journey uses your device's location while you are using the app and, when location tracking is active, while the app is in the background.

Location is used to determine which fixed Level-17 map squares you have visited and to display your explored territory.

The app does not use location information for advertising or marketing.

## Information stored on your device

Mapping the Journey stores exploration information locally on your device. This includes:

- identifiers of map squares you have visited;
- the date and time associated with recorded visits;
- visit counts;
- downloaded geographical information associated with visited squares;
- locally cached score and application state required for the app to operate.

This information is stored in local application databases on the device.

The current app does not maintain a continuous GPS route history in its visit database.

## Information sent to the Mapping the Journey development server

When the configured Mapping the Journey development server is available, the app may send:

- a development player identifier; and
- identifiers of visited map squares that require geographical resolution or synchronisation.

The server may return geographical information, score information and synchronisation status associated with those squares.

The app does not need to send your raw GPS coordinates to the development server for this process. Square identifiers are derived on the device from your location.

The current TestFlight version uses a development server intended for testing and is not a public production service.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

Mapping the Journey does not currently contain third-party advertising, advertising tracking or analytics services.

The app does not sell personal information.

The app does not use information collected through the app to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Apple and TestFlight

When you install or test Mapping the Journey through Apple TestFlight, Apple may process information relating to TestFlight installation, device operation, crashes and beta feedback according to Apple's own privacy policies and TestFlight terms.

That information is handled by Apple and is separate from the information processed directly by Mapping the Journey.

## Data deletion

Because the current TestFlight version stores exploration history primarily on the device, deleting the app removes its locally stored application data from that device.

The current development version does not provide a public user-account system.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Mapping the Journey develops, particularly if user accounts, public server services, additional analytics or other online features are introduced.

The current version of the policy will describe the behaviour of the version of Mapping the Journey being distributed.

## Contact

Privacy questions about Mapping the Journey may be directed to the developer through the contact information provided with the TestFlight build or future App Store listing.
